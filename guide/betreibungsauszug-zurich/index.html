The Betreibungsauszug for newcomers

Almost every landlord in Zurich asks for a Betreibungsauszug, the extract from the debt collection register. Here is what it shows, how to order it in the city of Zurich, and what to send while you are still waiting for yours.

What the Betreibungsauszug is

The Betreibungsauszug (in full: Betreibungsregisterauszug) is an official document from the debt collection office (Betreibungsamt) where you live. It shows whether anyone has started debt collection proceedings against you at that address in the last five years. In Switzerland, a creditor can start such proceedings without going to court first, so landlords use the extract to check whether a tenant pays their bills.

What landlords want to see is an extract without entries. It is only issued in German.

How to order it in the city of Zurich

The city has 12 debt collection offices, one for each Kreis. You do not need to know which one is yours. There are three ways to order:

  • Online form of the city of Zurich. Fill in the form on stadt-zuerich.ch and upload a photo or scan of your ID (passport, ID card or Swiss residence permit). The form sends the request to the right office. It costs CHF 17 plus postage, and the extract comes by A-Post to your Swiss address.
  • At the counter. Go to the office for your Kreis with your ID. You usually get the extract within a few minutes. You pay in cash or by debit card.
  • Through Swiss Post. On betreibungsauskunft.ch you order online for CHF 26.90 and get a signed PDF by email, usually within 24 hours on working days. At a post office counter it costs CHF 35.

You cannot order by email or phone. If you live outside the city, order from the debt collection office of your municipality. Many of them also have an online form.

The PDF from Swiss Post is handy because you can attach it directly to online applications. The paper extract from the city you have to scan.

The problem for newcomers

The register is kept by the office where you are officially registered as a resident. As long as you have not registered at a Swiss address, there is nothing to extract. You can only order a Betreibungsauszug after you have registered with your Kreisbüro (or your municipality). In Zurich you have to do this within 14 days of moving in.

Your first extract will be clean, but short: it states that you have only lived at the address since a certain date. That is normal. Property managers in Zurich see this all the time with people who have just arrived.

If you live in a temporary or furnished flat and are registered there, you can already order the extract from that address. This is the fastest way to have one when the search starts.

What to send until you have one

If you are applying before you are registered, do not leave the gap unexplained. Add these documents instead:

  • A short note in the dossier explaining when you arrived and that the extract will follow.
  • Your employment contract showing your gross salary, or a confirmation from your employer.
  • Your residence permit, or the confirmation that it has been applied for.
  • A reference from your previous landlord. English is fine.
  • If you have one: a credit report from your home country. It is not an official replacement, but some landlords appreciate it.

For the note, one sentence in German is enough, for example:

«Ich bin am 1. Dezember 2026 in die Schweiz gezogen. Den Betreibungsauszug reiche ich nach, sobald ich angemeldet bin.»

(«I moved to Switzerland on 1 December 2026. I will send the debt collection extract as soon as I am registered.»)

Tips

  • Order the extract as soon as you are registered. Most property managers do not accept one older than three months, so order a new one if your search takes longer.
  • If you lived in another Swiss municipality during the last five years, some landlords ask for the extract from there too. Order it from the debt collection office of that municipality.
  • Check the extract when you receive it. If it shows an entry you do not know about, contact the debt collection office before you send it to anyone.
