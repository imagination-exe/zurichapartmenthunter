Zurich rent prices 2026 by district

What a 2-, 3- or 4-room flat costs in each of Zurich's 12 districts (Kreise), taken from the city's official 2026 rent survey. And the number most guides leave out: if you sign a new lease, you pay more than the average.

The short answer

In the city of Zurich, a typical 3-room flat on the private market costs CHF 1'950 a month, net. Half of all these flats cost between CHF 1'550 and CHF 2'525. A 2-room flat is at CHF 1'670, a 4-room flat at CHF 2'434.

One thing that confuses many newcomers: in Switzerland, rooms are counted without kitchen and bathroom. A «3-room flat» (3 Zimmer) is a living room plus two bedrooms.

Kreis 1, the old town, is the most expensive: CHF 2'855 for 3 rooms. Kreis 8 (Seefeld) follows with CHF 2'400. The cheapest is Kreis 12 (Schwamendingen) at CHF 1'576, then Kreis 11 and 10 at around CHF 1'800. For the same 3 rooms you pay about CHF 1'280 more per month in Kreis 1 than in Kreis 12.

Kreis 1 has few flats, so its figures are less precise than those of the large Kreise such as 11 or 9.

Why you will probably pay more

The survey covers all existing leases, including people who have lived in the same flat for 20 years. Their rents have barely moved. Anyone who moves in today pays the current market price. For 3-room flats on the private market, the city's data shows:

  • Lease older than 20 years: CHF 1'397
  • Lease 3 to 10 years old: CHF 2'018
  • Re-let in the last 2 years: CHF 2'250
  • New building, first tenants: CHF 3'183

New leases are about 15 % above the overall median. When you set your budget, add roughly that much to the Kreis figures in the table. This is our own estimate based on the city-wide ratio. The city does not publish new-lease rents per Kreis.

Net rent and gross rent

All figures here are net rent (Nettomiete). Listings usually show the gross rent (Bruttomiete) or net rent plus service charges (Nebenkosten), which cover heating, hot water and shared building costs. For a 3-room flat on the private market the median gross rent is CHF 2'170, so plan for around CHF 220 on top of the net rent. Electricity and internet are usually extra.

Cooperative flats: half the price, long waiting lists

Flats from cooperatives (Genossenschaften), foundations and the city are much cheaper. The median for 3 rooms is CHF 1'066. Most cooperatives have waiting lists, require membership and set rules on how many people live in a flat. For your first flat in Zurich this is rarely an option, but it is worth applying once you are settled.

What income do you need?

Many landlords want the gross rent to be no more than about a third of your gross monthly income. Example: a 3-room flat in Kreis 3 with a new lease costs around CHF 2'170 net (CHF 1'883 + 15 %). With service charges that is about CHF 2'390. Landlords will then expect a household income of around CHF 7'200 gross per month. The rent calculator on our homepage does this for any salary.

The 12 Kreise at a glance

  • Kreis 1 (Rathaus, Hochschulen, Lindenhof, City): Old town and city centre. Few flats, most of them small. 3 rooms: CHF 2'855.
  • Kreis 2 (Enge, Wollishofen, Leimbach): Lakeside south of the centre, quieter and greener. 3 rooms: CHF 2'272.
  • Kreis 3 (Alt-Wiedikon, Friesenberg, Sihlfeld): Central and popular, from Lochergut to the foot of the Uetliberg. 3 rooms: CHF 1'883.
  • Kreis 4 (Werd, Langstrasse, Hard): Around Langstrasse, close to the main station. Busy, with lots of nightlife. 3 rooms: CHF 1'986.
  • Kreis 5 (Gewerbeschule, Escher Wyss): Zurich West, the former industrial quarter, with many newer buildings. 3 rooms: CHF 2'378.
  • Kreis 6 (Unterstrass, Oberstrass): Residential area close to ETH and the university. 3 rooms: CHF 2'120.
  • Kreis 7 (Fluntern, Hottingen, Hirslanden, Witikon): On the slopes of the Zürichberg. Green, with many larger flats. 3 rooms: CHF 2'230.
  • Kreis 8 (Seefeld, Mühlebach, Weinegg): Seefeld, on the eastern lakeshore. One of the most sought-after areas. 3 rooms: CHF 2'400.
  • Kreis 9 (Albisrieden, Altstetten): Large residential area in the west with a lot of new construction. 3 rooms: CHF 1'953.
  • Kreis 10 (Höngg, Wipkingen): Along the Limmat river, residential, with Höngg up on the hill. 3 rooms: CHF 1'804.
  • Kreis 11 (Affoltern, Oerlikon, Seebach): The biggest Kreis. Fast trains from Oerlikon to the main station and the airport. 3 rooms: CHF 1'801.
  • Kreis 12 (Saatlen, Schwamendingen-Mitte, Hirzenbach): The cheapest Kreis, with many cooperative flats. 3 rooms: CHF 1'576.

And outside the city?

The survey only covers the city of Zurich. Many people look in towns nearby, such as Schlieren, Wallisellen, Dübendorf or Adliswil, where there is less competition and the train takes 8 to 15 minutes to the main station. You can find a selection in the areas section of our homepage.
