What we do

  • We build your complete rental dossier, in the order Zurich property managers expect.
  • We write every application letter in German and adapt it to each apartment.
  • We check new listings every day, including housing cooperatives and property managers that only publish on their own websites. You get the ones that match your budget and areas.
  • We meet once in Zurich to go through your wishes and your documents.
  • You reach us by email and WhatsApp, and your messages come first.
  • You also get the complete Zurich Apartment Kit.

What you do

  • Send us your documents: passport or permit, employment contract or payslips, and the Betreibungsauszug once you have it.
  • Go to the viewings. Most landlords want to meet the people who will live in the flat.
  • Decide which apartment you take and sign the lease.

How it works

  1. Send a request with the form. We reply by email within 2 working days.
  2. We agree on the details by email and you receive an invoice. Your employer can pay it.
  3. We start with your dossier and meet in Zurich. From then on we search for you for 4 weeks.

Who it's for

People who start a job in Zurich soon and have little time. Families, because larger flats get the most applications. And anyone who doesn't speak German and doesn't want to write to property managers in German.

What we can't promise

We are not a real estate agency, and nobody can honestly guarantee you an apartment, because the landlord decides. What we make sure of: you apply to every suitable apartment quickly, with a complete dossier and a letter in proper German.

Questions

Can my employer pay for it?

Yes. Many companies have a relocation budget. We can invoice your company directly.

I'm not in Zurich yet. Can I still book?

Yes. We start with your documents and the dossier by email. We meet once you're in Zurich.

Do you make phone calls?

No. We work by email and WhatsApp, plus one meeting in Zurich. That way you have everything in writing.

Are there any other costs?

No. CHF 1'490 is the full price. We take no commission and no share of the rent.

Ready to hand over your search?

We take on a maximum of 3 clients per month. Tell us about your search, and we'll reply by email within 2 working days.
