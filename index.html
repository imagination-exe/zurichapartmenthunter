What exactly is in the kit?

A step-by-step guide, a Swiss-standard dossier template, German cover letter templates for different situations (single, couple, family, newcomer), email templates for agencies, a viewing checklist, an area guide and a German–English rental glossary.

I don't speak German. Can I still use it?

Yes. Every German template comes with an English explanation, and you only add 2–3 personal sentences. The kit is available in English, German, French, Italian and Spanish.

Do you guarantee me an apartment?

No one can honestly guarantee that, because the landlord decides. The kit makes you one of the strongest applicants. If it doesn't help you, you get your money back within 14 days.

I'm not in Switzerland yet. Is it still useful?

That's the best time. You can prepare your full dossier before you land and start applying on day one.

What is a Betreibungsauszug?

An extract from the debt collection register that shows you have no unpaid debts. Almost every landlord asks for it. The kit explains how to get it, even if you just arrived.

Can my employer pay for this?

Often yes. Many companies offer a relocation budget. You receive a proper invoice that you can submit.
